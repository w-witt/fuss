Fuss

Listen to math-heavy papers like an audiobook.

Drop an academic PDF and Fuss reads it aloud — equations and all — with word-by-word read-along. The Nougat model and the speech run on your own CPU/GPU; the file never leaves your machine. Beta

Why “Fuss”?

📄
Drop a PDF here or click to browse

First run downloads the model (~one time, then cached). For best speed use a browser with WebGPU (Chrome or Edge). Big or scanned PDFs take longer on CPU.

🔊 The voice comes from your browser/OS. This beta tests reading accuracy (especially math) — not voice quality.

Plain text & downloads

How it works

  1. Render — each PDF page is rasterized in your browser (pdf.js).
  2. Recognize — the Nougat neural model reads each page into LaTeX/Markdown, on your CPU or GPU.
  3. Speak-ready — an open rule library turns LaTeX into plain English (so "x^2" is read "x squared"). Your feedback improves it.
  4. Read aloud — your browser speaks the paper while highlighting each word on the PDF itself. Click anywhere on the page to start listening from there.

🔒 Nothing is uploaded. The conversion happens entirely on your device.
